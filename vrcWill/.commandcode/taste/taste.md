## Documentation
- Values exhaustive, detail-rich specs/docs: when asked to produce a design/spec document, wants maximum detail and completeness rather than a minimal draft (explicitly asks for "尽可能包含更多的细节"). Confidence: 0.7

Confidence: 0.6
